Hector Capital 💰
A lightweight, smart financial companion to track where every penny goes.

About
Welcome to Hector Capital, the second official project under the Hector company umbrella.

We’ve all been there: money slips through our fingers, and at the end of the month, we have no idea where it all went. Heavy financial software is often too complex and overwhelming for everyday use. That’s why we built Hector Capital—a clean, fast, and minimalist tool designed to make tracking your daily expenses effortless.

Key Features
Simple Expense Tracking: Log what you spend and what it was for in seconds.

Smart Breakdown: Instantly see how much of your total budget has been used and where your money is flowing.

Lightweight & Fast: No clutter, no complex databases—just pure, efficient code that gets the job done.
